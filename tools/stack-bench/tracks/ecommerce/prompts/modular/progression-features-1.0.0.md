## Faceted search

Let visitors narrow the catalogue by category, price range, and availability. Filters work
together. Results are ordered by name and shown six per page without missing or repeated items.

## Personalized recommendations

Show signed-in customers recommendations based on their own activity. Prefer items in categories
the customer has purchased from. Break ties by global units sold and then by item name. One
customer's activity must not change another customer's personal profile.

## Delivery notifications

Notify a customer when one of their orders is delivered. A delivery creates one notification,
and only the order owner can see it.

## Automatic reorder rules

Let warehouse staff define a stock threshold and restock quantity for an item. Crossing the
threshold schedules one restock. The rule must not schedule duplicates while one is pending.

## Cart recovery

Let a signed-in customer restore an expired cart. Restore only quantities that can still be
reserved and clearly show anything that could not be restored.

## Recommendation feedback

Let a customer dismiss a recommendation. It stays dismissed for that customer while other
customers remain unaffected.
