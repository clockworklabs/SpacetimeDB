# Fulfilment operations

Add a fulfilment area for staff and administrators.

- New orders appear in the queue without a reload. Show the item and the warehouse that will ship it.
- Staff and administrators can mark a pending order as shipped. Remove it from every open queue and update the customer's order history without a reload.
- Customers cannot open the fulfilment area or mark an order as shipped. Enforce this rule on the server.
- A customer cannot change another customer's order. Enforce this rule on the server.
