# Operational views

Give staff and administrators live views of the store's current orders and stock:

- **Low stock:** items with 10 units or fewer, most urgent first.
- **Warehouse utilisation:** the total units held by each warehouse.
- **Category totals:** units sold and revenue for each category.
- **Fulfilment queue depth:** the number of orders waiting to ship.
- **Recommended for you:** items from categories the customer has bought from, excluding items already in the cart. Signed-out visitors see best sellers.

Each view updates when its underlying orders or stock change. Everyone viewing the same data sees the same result.
