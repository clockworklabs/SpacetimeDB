## Customer profile

A signed-in customer can save their name and shipping address. The profile persists and is
private to that customer.

## Staff roles

Administrators can assign staff roles. A staff member can use only the areas allowed by their
role. Staff members cannot assign roles.

## Catalog management

Authorized staff can add a product with named variants. Visitors can see the product and choose
from its variants.

## Payment records

Checkout records the amount paid on the order. Retrying the same checkout does not create a
second payment.

## Staff activity history

Authorized staff can inspect a history of administrative changes. Each entry identifies the
staff member, action, subject, and time. Customers cannot open this history.
