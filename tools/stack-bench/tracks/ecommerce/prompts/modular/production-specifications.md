## automatic-reorder-access

Only warehouse staff can manage automatic reorder rules.

## recommendation-profile-isolation

Customer recommendation profiles are isolated.

## staff-activity-privacy

Customers cannot open staff activity history.

## order-support-ownership

A customer cannot attach or inspect another customer's order.

## delivery-notification-privacy

Delivery notifications are private to the order owner.

## support-refund-access

Only authorized staff can refund an order from a support case.

## recommendation-feedback-privacy

Recommendation feedback belongs to one customer.

## recommendation-feedback-restart

Dismissed recommendations stay dismissed after reconnecting or restarting the server.

## automatic-reorder-deduplication

A pending automatic restock is not scheduled twice.

## payment-deduplication

A checkout does not create duplicate payments.

## support-refund-accounting

The recorded refund equals the amount paid and is applied only once.
