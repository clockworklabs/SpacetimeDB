## Scheduled purchases

Customers can subscribe to an individual catalog item (not a bundle) and quantity for a chosen number of deliveries at a chosen interval. Use the item price at subscription creation. The first delivery is due after one interval. Each delivery creates an ordinary order and payment. Skip an unavailable delivery without charging; it still uses one delivery slot. Customers can pause, resume, or cancel future deliveries; a pause moves future due times by the pause duration.
