## Staff access

Staff and administrators can sign in. Staff areas are not available to customers.

## Support intake

Anyone can open a support ticket with contact details, a subject, and a message. Return a
reference that can be used to check the ticket.

## Support triage

Staff can view new tickets, assign them, set priority, and change their status.

## Customer support history

Signed-in customers can see support tickets that belong to their account. They cannot see
another customer's tickets.

## Managed support cases

When a customer ticket and a staff assignment refer to the same case, both sides see the same
status and replies.

## Order-linked support

A customer can attach one of their orders to a support case. Staff can inspect that order from
the case. A customer cannot attach another customer's order.

## Support refunds

Authorized staff can resolve an order-linked case with a refund. The order, refund total, case,
and customer view must agree.

## Promotion rules

Staff can create a promotion with a code, discount, active period, and usage limit.

## Promotion checkout

A customer can apply an active promotion to a cart. The server calculates the discount and
records it on the order. Expired or exhausted promotions are refused.

## Promotion reporting

Staff can see exact redemption counts and discounted revenue for each promotion.

## Notification preferences

Signed-in customers can choose which notifications they receive. Their choices persist and are
private to their account.

## Stock alerts

A customer can request an alert for an unavailable item. The alert is sent once when stock
returns and only to customers who requested it.
